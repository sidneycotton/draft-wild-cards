How to Play

1

Open Packs

Rip open your packs! Every card you open gets added to a big pool that you can use to build your deck.

2

Wild Shinies

Packs may contain an extra shiny card. It can be of ANY rarity. Muahahaha, totally wild! 😈

3

Build Your Deck

Choose 1 Hero and exactly your required amount of normal cards from the pool to create your final deck strategy.

4

Battle!

To play, you need to have someone else also draft a deck and match up with them in a custom game inside Wild Cards.

1

Start with 1000 Credits

You have a budget to purchase any combination of packs you desire from the shop.

2

Drafting Packs

Each pack has a distinct price and unique set of drops. Manage your credits wisely to build the best pool possible.

3

Finish Purchasing

When you have run out of credits, or are happy with the pool you've accumulated, click Done.

4

Choose Your Hero & Build

After finalizing your pool, you will proceed directly to deckbuilding where you select 1 of 3 random Heroes.
